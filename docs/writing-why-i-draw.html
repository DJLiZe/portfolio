Why I Draw (EXAMPLE)

September 2026 · excerpt

I started drawing because I was bored in class. That is the honest answer, and it is not a very impressive one. A pencil, the back of a worksheet, and forty minutes of a lecture I had already read about: that was the beginning.

What kept me drawing was something different. When I draw a thing, I have to actually look at it. Not glance - look. How the light sits on the edge of a cup. How a friend's shoulders drop when they finally relax. Drawing taught me that most of the time I am only half paying attention to the world, and that paying full attention is a skill you can practice.

This portfolio is where I keep that practice: the finished pieces, the experiments, and the writing that grew out of the looking.
